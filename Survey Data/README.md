Survey Methodology

A perceptual survey was conducted to assess whether colour differences between three sections of a paint swatch were visually detectable. The swatch had been exposed to different light conditions for three months: one section was fully covered (control), one was covered with UV-filtering acrylic (TruVue Optium Museum Acrylic®, UV 99%), and one was left fully exposed to daylight.

Participants with no prior knowledge of the experiment were randomly recruited and asked to visually examine the paint swatch. They were free to hold and inspect the sample as they wished before comparing the three sections and reporting whether visible colour differences were present using a three-level scale (no visible difference, somewhat visible, very visible). Participants also indicated which section appeared most different.

Participant responses were anonymised before inclusion in the dataset.
